In the [[Guildocracy]] a governor gets elected every 4 years, and there is a maximum of 2 terms of being governor. The governor’s job is to mediate between the [[Guild Leaders]] and to make sure that their decisions benefit everyone.

the Governor does hold some power, if leaders are tied on a decision the governor may vote as a tiebreaker, secondly a governor may veto a decision, the veto is stopped if the majority of guilds vote to proceed with the decision. Thirdly the governor may call a vote upon all relevant guild leaders to make them vote upon a decision of one leader. Lastly the governor may delay a decision, the maximum allowed delay is 30 days.
