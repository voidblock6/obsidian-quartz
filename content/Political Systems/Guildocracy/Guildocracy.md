The guildocracy is the political system of the [[Cindrisian Archipelago]], it was formed in the [[Political shift]] a movement after the [[Volashnic Revolution]].

The guildocracy is a semi-democratic political system, every 2 years guilds hold a vote for their guild leader, only members of that guild can vote in the election and someone can only become leader for a maximum of 3 terms.

Besides having guild leaders the guildocracy also has a governor, the governor is voted upon every 4 years, all people of the archipelago may vote. There is a maximum of 2 terms for being a governor.

In the guildocracy the guild  leaders hold a lot of power, together they rule in the [[Assembly of leaders]], leaders choose what happens to their guild to some extent, for some decisions the assembly is called upon. The governor’s job is to mediate between the leaders and make sure that they make decisions that benefit all of the archipelago.
