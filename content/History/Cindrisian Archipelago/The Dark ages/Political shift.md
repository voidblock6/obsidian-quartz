After the [[Volashnic Revolution]] the people of the [[Cindrisian Archipelago]] wanted a better form of government, no more kings’s and council’s. They wanted the people that govern them to be elected.

The best solution they found was called the [[Guildocracy]], the first system where the people chose their leaders, they chose a [[Governor]] and a guild leader to represent them in the [[Assembly of leaders]]
