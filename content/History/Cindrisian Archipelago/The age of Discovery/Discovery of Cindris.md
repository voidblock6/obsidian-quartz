[[Cindris]] was discovered around 2 millennia ago, fisherman found the paradise that was the [[Volashnic Sea]] and eventually stumbled upon Cindris, over the following months the news about Cindris quickly spread and fisherman and merchants alike started coming to Cindris.

As more people arrived over the years a village started forming on the southwestern coast of the island, another few years later [[Founding of Ashport|Ashport was founded]] there. [[Ashport]] later became the capital city of Cindris.
