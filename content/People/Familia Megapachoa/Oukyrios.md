Oukyrios was a member of Weave Lord family [[Familia Megapachoa]]. Born around half a millenium ago he lived for around 105 years. 

The name "Oukyrios" means sky lord in [[Telacallian]], his parents chose this name because they believed he would fly high and achieve great things, he lived up to the name. The name Oukyrios is reserved solely for the upper class, no Magistri or Operarius may call themselves a lord. 

In the time that he lived he was an influential person, he founded the company [[Weaveforge Industries]] which produces all kinds of woven goods in its factory in [[Silkmist]] and still stands strong to this day. As a Weave Lord he was respected by the queen and the lower caste, as far as respect goes for the lower caste.
