Thalakyrios is a member of [[Familia Megapachoa]], he was born around 43 years ago and still has a long life ahead of him.

The name "Thalakyrios" means sea lord in [[Telacallian]], his parents were into symbolism, so they chose this because they hoped he would one day inherit [[Weaveforge Industries]], which made him the person responsible for keeping [[Volashnic Sea]] water cleanable. The name Thalakyrios is reserved solely for the upper class, no Magistri or Operarius may call themselves a lord. 

Thalakyrios inherited Weaveforge Industries around 17 years ago, the company has thrived under his leadership and may even be stronger than ever.
