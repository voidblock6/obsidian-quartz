Kiylian was the last king of the [[Cindrisian Archipelago]], he was crowned at age 27 after his father stepped down from the throne because of the dissatisfaction of the people that he didn't know a fix for.

Kiylian tried fixing the issues but he was seeing the monarchy crumble and decided the best way forward was to end the monarchy before a revolution started, he transferred power in the [[Council's Pact]], an agreement to end the monarchy and give power to a council of influential merchants, in return the excessive loans his house took on over the decades would be voided. 

After the peaceful transfer of power he was never to be seen again, it was said he went on to travel the world like his ancestors.
