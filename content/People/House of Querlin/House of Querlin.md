The house of Querlin is a millennia old family that has had a significant impact on the history of [[Cindrisian Archipelago]], the 11th heir, [[Vander Xan]] settled on [[Cindris]] and founded [[Ashport]], the first city, he was promptly crowned king of Cindris.

[[Kiylian Reyn]] the 28th heir ended the monarchy in the [[Council's Pact]], a peaceful transfer of power after the house took excessive loans and failed to repay them.
