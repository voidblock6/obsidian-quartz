Vander was a rich merchant who heard rumors of a paradise island with the name [[Cindris]], He and the rest of the House of Querlin set to sea after verifying these rumors and eventually got to the island.

Just like most settlers he the and rest of the house settled closely to the shore of Cindris, his riches enabled the quick [[Founding of Ashport]] and building of infrastructure, the rich fishers and merchants later offered Vander to become king of Cindris, he accepted. As king he ordered building of a castle on the tiny island of the shore of Cindris, he named the island [[King's Isle]].  He and his house lived there for the rest of his life and Vander died at age 89.
