Red Ember Haven, the main haven of the coastal city of [[Ashport]], it lies besides the highly dangerous [[Volashnic Sea]]. The haven is one of the biggest of its kind, having hundreds of ships docking and leaving it every day

The Ashportians earn most of their money from transporting goods to and from the haven, like their famous [[Volashnic Brew]]. Although that's not their only reason for sailing across the dangerous sea, they need to because they have to trade  [[Spidersilk Filter|Spidersilk Filters]] with the people of [[Telacallia]] so they can
clean their water of toxins. The filters are transported all across [[Cindris]] after arriving in the haven
