Ashport, the capital city of the island of [[Cindris]], its a city near the southwestern coast, bordering the [[Volashnic Sea]] and the [[Aquashnic Sea]]

Ashport was a paradise with life and plants everywhere but because of a major event called the [[Volashnic Rift]] it has turned into a toxic and dangerous sea. Now the Ashportians need to refine their water using [[King's Crossing]] and the [[Water Refinery]] to survive, refining it requires a [[Spidersilk Filter|Spidersilk Filters]]which they get by trading with [[Telacallia]] and bringing it into [[Red Ember Haven]].

Ashport is a trading hub, it harbors people from all around the world, you might find some of those people inside the [[Ashblack Inn]] drinking Ashports local specialty: [[Volashnic Brew]].
