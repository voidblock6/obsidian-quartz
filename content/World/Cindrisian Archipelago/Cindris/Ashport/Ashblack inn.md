The Ashblack Inn, the place where sailors and townsfolk meet after a busy day, located right across the street from [[Red Ember Haven]]. The inn is famous for its [[Volashnic Brew]]: a beer grown with ingredients watered with water from the [[Volashnic Sea]]. 

The inn is owned by the renowned Vin Vantablack, a 57 year old man famous for owning The Ashblack inn and his wacky food innovations.
