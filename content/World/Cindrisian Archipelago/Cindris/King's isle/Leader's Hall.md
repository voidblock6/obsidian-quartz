Leader's hall is a grand circular building, located on [[King's Isle]].

Inside the grand building is a large room, in the middle of the room there is a circular table that has 20 chairs for small discussions. During larger discussions the table is mechanically raised to turn it into a podium where leaders debate, discuss or share their opinions and ideas. Around the table there are 12 benches where the leaders sit, each with 9 seats.

The roof of the building is an impressive dome of glass and brass. During  meetings the iris mechanism closes, plunging the room into darkness, above the podium sunlight shines through a small hole in the iris to put it in the spotlight. When a leader speaks the governor activates a series of mirrors to put that leader's seat in the spotlight to show that its their turn to speak.
