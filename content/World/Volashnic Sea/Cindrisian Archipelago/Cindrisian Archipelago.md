The biggest archipelago in the [[Volashnic Sea]], it consists of around [[PLACEHOLDER]] islands and has a total population of around [[PLACEHOLDER]] people, with the biggest island, [[Cindris]] having the majority of the population and the capital, [[Ashport]].  

The Cindrisian Archipelago harbors many species and cultures because it originally was a trading hub and fishing hotspot in the time of its discovery around 2 millennia ago. Throughout the long history of the archipelago there have been a fair number of conflicts between cultures and species but thankfully it is relatively peaceful in this day and age. 

The archipelago also has developed its own political system after they were done with centuries of kings and councils failing to rule their country, their solution: the [[Guildocracy]], a system where everyone has a vote and nobody holds too much power for too long, a system they deem fair.

The climate of most of the islands in the archipelago was originally temperate and tropical but turned into a temperate desert without plants and animals because of the toxic water caused by the [[Volashnic Rift]]. there are some small areas with clean water that do have flora and fauna.
