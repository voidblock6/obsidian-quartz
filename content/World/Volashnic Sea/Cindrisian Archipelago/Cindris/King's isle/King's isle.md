King's isle is home to the governor of [[Cindris]], its a relatively small island just off the coast of Cindris, its connect to [[Ashport]] by the [[King's Crossing]], a bridge that doubles as part of the market.

Historically King’s isle first served as a home for the [[House of Querlin]], who’s bloodline ruled Cindris for around 800 years. Then it served as the home of The Council after the monarchy ended, eventually the reign of The Council ended too and a democracy was formed, today King’s isle serves as a home for the governor and the rest of the government, in celebration of the democracy King’s crossing was built.
