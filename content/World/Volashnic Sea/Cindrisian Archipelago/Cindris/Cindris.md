The biggest island of the [[Cindrisian Archipelago]], located inside [[Volashnic Sea]]. Cindris has around [[PLACEHOLDER]] inhabitants with the majority of them living in the capital, [[Ashport]]. 

Cindris was originally a mix of a temperate and tropical landscape, after the [[Volashnic Rift]] the landscape turned into a temperate desert without flora and fauna, some small areas still harbor life because of rare non-toxic water sources. The biggest habitable area is found around Ashport, since the Ashportians devised ways to clean their water they used it to reclaim some areas of land.
