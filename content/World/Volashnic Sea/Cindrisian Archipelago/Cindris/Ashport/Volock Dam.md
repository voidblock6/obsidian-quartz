Volock dam is a crucial piece of infrastructure keeping the [[Aquashnic Sea]] from getting contaminated with [[Volashnic Sea]] water and keeps the sea accessible for ships.

The dam spans from the mainland of [[Cindris]] to [[King's isle]], the dam has a large canal lock which allows ships to move between the two seas and get to [[Red Ember Haven]]. 

The construction of Volock dam started at the same time as [[King's Crossing]] and was finished after around 5 years of work, a few years before King's crossing because of it being significantly less complicated, Volock dam and King's crossing both keep the Aquashnic Sea contained.
