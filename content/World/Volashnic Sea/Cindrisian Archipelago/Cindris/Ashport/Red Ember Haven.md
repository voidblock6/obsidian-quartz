Red Ember Haven based in [[Ashport]] and borders the [[Aquashnic Sea]], it is one of the biggest havens on the entire continent. the haven is one of the main sources of income for the entire [[Cindrisian Archipelago]] since of trading being the biggest industry.

The haven is also where [[Spidersilk Filter|Spidersilk Filters]] imported from [[Telacallia]]  get delivered, which makes the haven vital to the survival of the island. Many other things are imported and exported from the haven, from food like [[Volashnic Brew]] to valuable metals like gold. 

Since sailing across the [[Volashnic Sea]] is highly dangerous because of its unpredictability, navigators are required to be able to avoid the hazards the sea has to offer. Almost all ships that enter the haven are Cindrisian ships since they pick up all traded goods themselves because most other civilizations can't. They sail them across the Volashnic Sea and pass through [[Volock Dam]] to enter the Aquashnic Sea and dock at the haven.
