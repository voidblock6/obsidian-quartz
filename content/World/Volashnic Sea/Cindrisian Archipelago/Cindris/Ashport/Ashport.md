Ashport is the capital city of the [[Cindrisian Archipelago]] and is the biggest city of the archipelago harboring around [[PLACEHOLDER]] people. Ashport resides on the southwestern coast of the largest island of the archipelago, [[Cindris]].

Ashportians are great engineers and inventors, when the water of the [[Volashnic Sea]] turned toxic after the [[Volashnic Rift]] they engineered [[King's Crossing]], a bridge that cleans the water from most toxins and particles, the water is then dumped into the [[Aquashnic Sea]], a man  made sea contained by King's crossing and [[Volock Dam]]. This water was clean enough for plants to start growing again making Ashport have a nice temperate climate, to make the water drinkable they had to refine it a second time inside the [[Water Refinery]]

Ashport is one of the biggest trading hubs on the entire continent of [[PLACEHOLDER]], [[Red Ember Haven]], the main haven of Ashport is located on a coast bordering the Aquashnic Sea and is located close to Volock Dam. Red Ember Haven is also crucial to the survival of the entire island because it is where the [[Spidersilk Filter|Spidersilk Filters]] imported from [[Telacallia]] arrived, which are used inside King's crossing and the Water Refinery to clean the water.
