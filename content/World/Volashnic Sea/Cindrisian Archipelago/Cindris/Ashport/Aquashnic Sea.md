The Aquashnic sea is located between [[King's isle]] and the mainland of [[Cindris]], the sea is full of water that has been filtered by [[King's Crossing]], the water is clean enough for plants to be able to grow from it but its still too toxic for land animals and the inhabitants of [[Ashport]].

The water from the Aquashnic Sea comes from King's crossing which takes the water from the [[Volashnic Sea]] and cleans the bulk of it, on the other side there is [[Volock Dam]] which acts as a dam and is also a canal lock for ships to pass through and reach [[Red Ember Haven]].

Some species of fish have accustomed to the water of the Aquashnic sea and the sea has grown its own unique ecosystem, fishing is allowed under heavy regulations.
