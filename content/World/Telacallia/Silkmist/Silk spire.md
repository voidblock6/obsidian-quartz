*Serhiuitzillia*

The Silk spire is the biggest temple of it's kind, its a place to worship the gods of [[Arachnism]], [[Deus Araneus]] and [[Thea Arachnea]]. The temple is located on top of a spire in the middle of the silkmist cave system.

The temple has a big ring shaped building, inside there is a garden that is filled with flora on one half,  and filled with gravel on the other half. Both halves have a small part of their counterpart in them, just like yin and yang, showing the sacred balance between the gods. In the middle of the garden stands a grand altar where rituals are performed, like the [[Ritual of Sol and Selena]] that is performed at sun- and moonrise.
