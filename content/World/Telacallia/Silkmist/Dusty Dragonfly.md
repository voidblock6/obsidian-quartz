***pulvepipiyollotlia***

The dusty dragonfly is a popular food stand in [[Silkmist]] that sells [[Salt-Steamed Dragonfly|Salt-Steamed Dragonflies]], a [[Story/World/Telacallia/Telacallia|Telacallian]] delicacy made by salting dragonflies and them steaming them in salted water. The foodstand is located on [[PLACEHOLDER]] and is popular under the working class of the city because of the food being well made and fairly priced.
