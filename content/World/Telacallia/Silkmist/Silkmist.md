Silkmist, the capital of [[Telacallia]]. A massive underground city located under mount Seriyohual. it's home to mainly [[Aranea Anthroposis]] but some humans do live there. Silkmist is a unique city, most cities are 1 brood but Silkmist is made of 3 broods and thus its ran by 3 Aranea Reginae, for the past century it has been  peaceful but it wasn't always like this.
