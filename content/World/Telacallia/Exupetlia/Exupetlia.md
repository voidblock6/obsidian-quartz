*Exupetlia*

Exupetlia, commonly referred to as Exile city. Exile city is a carved inside mount Obscutlayohualli, It's isolated from the rest of society and home to [[Aranea Anthroposis|Aranea's]] exiled from their brood. The city doesn't have any Aranea Reginae to rule over them, instead it is an almost lawless society ran by crime lords.
