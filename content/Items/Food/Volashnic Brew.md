the signature brew from the [[Ashblack Inn]] popular in and around the [[Ashport]] area. it has a slightly earthy and spicy taste with hints of sweetness from the prickly pear in it, some hate it some love it but one thing is clear: its not for the weak. Its very popular & valuable because of its unique taste and its rumored ability to mysteriously heal people from the common cold.
Some people think the herbs gave it the healing properties others think its because the recipe uses hop grown with water from the [[Volashnic Sea]].
The exact recipe is a secret closely guarded by a select group of people trusted by the owner of The Ashblack inn. 
Some stories claim that those who tried to replicate the brew using untreated Volashnic Sea water met unfortunate fates, though the truth of these tales is unknown.
