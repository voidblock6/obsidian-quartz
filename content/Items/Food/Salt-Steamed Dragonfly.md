Salt-Steamed Dragonfly is a [[Story/World/Telacallia/Telacallia|Telacallian]] delicacy, especially popular under the working class. The delicacy is  prepared by first salting the dragonflies and then steaming them, some recipes require the water used to steam the dragonflies to be mixed with salt to increase the flavour, although its unknown if it really does anything.

The salt used for making Salt-Steamed Dragonflies is imported from [[Ashport]], since they have a surplus of salt it's a really cheap good and the transport costs next to nothing since Ashportians usually bring some when they travel to Telacallia to get [[Spidersilk Filter|Spidersilk Filters]]
